{%- comment -%} Picks the posts of a resource (newest first) and assigns them to `feed_auswahl`. Used by the pages (buttons) and by their feeds (_plugins/beitrag_feeds.rb). Parameters: res= the resource art="projects" posts with the project's event_tag in `tags` (as "Stand / Updates") art="team" posts with the person in `author` (a slug or a list) `feed_eintraege` = number of entries in the feed (for projects including manual `updates`). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign fa_res = include.res -%} {%- assign feed_auswahl = "" | split: "" -%} {%- if include.art == "projects" -%} {%- if fa_res.event_tag -%}{%- for po in site.posts -%}{%- if po.tags contains fa_res.event_tag -%}{%- assign feed_auswahl = feed_auswahl | push: po -%}{%- endif -%}{%- endfor -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign feed_eintraege = feed_auswahl.size | plus: fa_res.updates.size -%} {%- elsif include.art == "team" -%} {%- for po in site.posts -%}{%- assign po_au = po.author | join: "," | split: "," -%}{%- if po_au contains fa_res.slug -%}{%- assign feed_auswahl = feed_auswahl | push: po -%}{%- endif -%}{%- endfor -%} {%- assign feed_eintraege = feed_auswahl.size -%} {%- endif -%}
